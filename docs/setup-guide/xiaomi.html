Saraswati · Setup on Xiaomi

These steps are for Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones. Your organiser is on the video call with you. Menu names change a little between Android versions. If you cannot find one, type its name into the search bar at the top of Settings, or ask the organiser.

1. Messages

You need two private messages from the organiser: this guide, and a pairing link. Do not open the pairing link yet. You use it in step 4.

2. Install Saraswati Updates

Saraswati Updates is a small helper app. It installs Saraswati and keeps it up to date.

  1. Open Chrome and go to https://github.com/diamondspark/saraswati/releases/latest.
  2. Under Assets, tap the file whose name starts with saraswati-updater and ends in .apk. Not the one that ends in .sha256, and not the one that is only called saraswati. If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Keep.
  3. When the download finishes, tap Open. Android says your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from Chrome. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then press back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

3. Install Saraswati

  1. Saraswati Updates offers to install Saraswati. Follow its prompts. The buttons say Allow installing (one time) and then Install.
  2. When Saraswati Updates says All done. Now switch the permission off., do exactly that. Switch Install unknown apps off for Saraswati Updates, and for Chrome too.
    • Where: usually Settings, Privacy protection, Special permissions, Install unknown apps.
    • Or type "Install unknown apps" into the search bar at the top of Settings.
  3. Xiaomi only Let Saraswati keep playing when the screen is locked. Open Settings, Apps, Manage apps, Saraswati, Battery saver, then choose No restrictions.
  4. Xiaomi only Let Saraswati start in the background. Open Settings, Apps, Permissions, Autostart, then switch Saraswati on.

4. Pair the phone

The organiser makes a one-time pairing link in Saraswati's Manage Family screen and sends it to you privately. It looks like this:

https://saraswati-sangat.pandey-mohitk.workers.dev/pair#<token>

The real link has a long string of 64 letters and numbers where <token> is. It is different every time, it works for one phone, once, and it stops working after 15 minutes. Never post it or send it on, and never put a real one in a message when asking for help.

  1. When the organiser says it is ready, tap the pairing link in the message. Saraswati opens on Join the family.
  2. Give the phone a name the organiser will recognise, and say who the phone is for: an adult, or a grandparent (दादा-दादी · नाना-नानी). Tap Send request.
  3. The organiser sees your phone in Manage Family and approves it. Wait. You do not need to do anything. Saraswati checks every few seconds.
  4. Sign in to YouTube inside Saraswati, with your own hands and your own password.
  5. Grandparent phones: pick favourite singers so the Saral home screen is ready to play. Hindi and Bollywood, devotional and bhajan, and ghazal are already ticked. Untick any you do not want.

The link opened a web page instead? If it says "Open this link on a phone that has Saraswati installed", Saraswati is not installed yet (finish step 3), or Android did not pass the link on. Open Saraswati, tap Family, tap Join the family, then copy the link, tap Paste, and tap Continue.

If the link says it has expired or was already used, ask the organiser for a new one.

5. Car and Google check

Android Auto (only if you drive with it)

  1. Open the Android Auto settings on the phone.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Version ten times. Accept the message that appears.
  3. Open the three-dot menu, then Developer settings, and switch on Unknown sources.

Without this, Android Auto hides apps that were not installed from the Play Store. It is Android Auto's own setting. It does not turn on your phone's Developer options.

Google developer verification

Google checks that apps outside the Play Store come from a registered developer, and each phone has to be authorised once. The organiser sends you a link or QR code from Google. Open it on this phone and confirm the message on the screen. Only use one that the organiser sent you themselves.

The organiser may ask you to do this before step 2. Either order works. The full procedure is in the developer verification notes.

When you are done

  • A song plays, and keeps playing with the screen locked.
  • Install unknown apps is off for Chrome, Saraswati Updates and Saraswati.

Saraswati is a private family app, not on the Play Store. If something here does not match your screen, stop and ask the organiser.

Saraswati · Setup guide

This guide puts Saraswati, our family music app, on your phone. Your family organiser walks through it with you on a video call, so you are never doing it alone.

Before you start

  • The organiser sends you two private messages: this guide, and a pairing link.
  • A pairing link looks like this, with a long string of letters and numbers in place of <token>: https://saraswati-sangat.pandey-mohitk.workers.dev/pair#<token>
  • Do not open the pairing link yet. It works for one phone, once, and stops working 15 minutes after the organiser makes it. They make it when you reach step 4.
  • Keep the pairing link to yourself. Do not post it or forward it.
  • If Play Protect asks to scan the app, let it. Never switch it off.
  • Sign in to YouTube yourself, on your own phone. Nobody needs your password, and you should never send it to anyone.

Pick your phone

  • Pixel Google Pixel, and most other Android phones
  • Samsung Galaxy phones and tablets
  • Xiaomi Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones

Another brand? Use the Pixel page. If music stops when the screen locks, tell the organiser, because some brands have extra battery settings.

The five steps

Each phone page has all five, with the exact menus for that brand.

  1. Messages. You have this guide and the pairing link. Join the video call.
  2. Install Saraswati Updates. A small helper app that installs and updates Saraswati. Samsung phones need Auto Blocker switched off for a few minutes first.
  3. Install Saraswati. Saraswati Updates does it. Then you switch the install permission off again, and set the battery so music keeps playing with the screen locked (Samsung and Xiaomi).
  4. Pair the phone. Open the pairing link in Saraswati and send the request, picking Adult. Keep the Join screen open until it says approved. Then sign in to YouTube. For a grandparent's phone, the organiser then switches it to the grandparent screen in Manage Family.
  5. Car and Google check. If you use Android Auto, switch on its "Unknown sources". Then the organiser sends you a link from Google to authorise this phone.

Saraswati is a private family app, not on the Play Store. If something here does not match your screen, stop and ask the organiser.
